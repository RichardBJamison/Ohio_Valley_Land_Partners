What we review
Vacant lots, inherited land, tax-burdened parcels, rural acreage, infill lots, landlocked or poor-access parcels, estate-owned land, parcels with title questions, no utilities, or severed minerals. Review is parcel-specific. Not every property receives an offer.

Where
Published acquisition pages: Franklin, Belmont, Jefferson, Columbiana, Harrison, and Carroll counties in Ohio; Ohio, Marshall, and Brooke counties in West Virginia. Guides also cover Geauga County, Ohio. Regional language includes Kentucky and Indiana. Other Ohio Valley counties may still submit an address. Focus varies by county and campaign.

Process
1. Owner shares property address plus phone and email.
2. OVLP reviews parcel, tax, access, title information, zoning, surroundings, and possible use.
3. We reach out promptly. Within a day or two we complete our review and, when it fits, bring comparable sales — transparent, no pressure.
4. If it fits, a written direct-purchase proposal may follow.
5. Direct cash closing; no agent; no listing commission; closing costs covered.
6. Typical close 45–90 days; as little as 30; 15 when truly needed. No guaranteed date on a specific file.

A review does not commit the owner to sell. A proposal does not require acceptance.

Owners do not need to solve title, taxes, access, or zoning first. Start with the address.

Other facts
- 5% of company profits committed to local community reinvestment as the business grows.
- Built on over two decades of real-estate experience, applied parcel by parcel.
- Financing partners (including Real Estate Capital Resources) may be used on OVLP acquisitions. OVLP is not a retail lender.
- Comparable-sales package is not an appraisal or statement of market value.
- Legal, tax, title, and sale-strategy questions belong with independent licensed professionals.
